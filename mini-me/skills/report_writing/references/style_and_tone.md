# Style And Tone

Write for clarity, not performance.

## Preferred style

- short paragraphs
- specific claims
- clear transitions
- plain, precise wording
- headings that reflect actual content

## Tone

- professional
- measured
- direct
- evidence-aware

## Avoid

- hype
- vague intensifiers
- false precision
- unnecessary jargon
- presenting caveats as footnotes when they materially affect conclusions

## Good practice

- say what was found
- say how strong the evidence is
- say what limits the claim
- say what should happen next

## Audience adaptation

For executive audiences:

- shorten method sections
- foreground implications
- keep caveats visible but compact

For technical audiences:

- include method rationale
- preserve important metric and modeling detail
- keep uncertainty language explicit
