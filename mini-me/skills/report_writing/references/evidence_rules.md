# Evidence Rules

Do not let one type of analysis make claims that belong to another.

## EDA

EDA supports:

- patterns
- distributions
- anomalies
- descriptive relationships

EDA does not by itself prove explanation or prediction quality.

## Diagnostic analytics

Diagnostic results support:

- plausible explanations
- adjusted associations
- evidence consistent with certain drivers

Diagnostic results do not automatically justify strong causal claims.

## Predictive analytics

Predictive results support:

- future-oriented estimates
- expected model performance under the chosen validation design
- uncertainty or calibration statements when available

Predictive performance does not prove causal explanation.

## Data cleaning

Cleaning results support:

- what was corrected
- what remained unresolved
- how trustworthy later analysis may be

Cleaning notes should inform confidence in all downstream findings.

## Research and references

Academic or external references support:

- contextualization
- comparison with prior work
- triangulation of findings

Do not use citations to overstate what the current dataset proves.

## Conflict handling

If upstream outputs conflict:

- say they conflict
- explain the type of conflict
- identify which interpretation is more cautious
- recommend follow-up instead of smoothing over the disagreement

## Missing-output rule

If an upstream section is missing:

- say so briefly
- do not fabricate content
