# Compliance And Attribution

Use this reference whenever the Asta tools are involved.

## Usage constraints

- Keys are granted for non-commercial research use only.
- Respect the rate limit associated with the key: each endpoint is limited to
  10 requests per second. If requests exceed that rate, the service may respond
  with status 429.
- Avoid unnecessary tool loops or broad brute-force retrieval.

## Attribution

If outputs are used in an application, site, or published material, preserve the
required Asta attribution and the required citation to the AstaBench paper when
applicable under the license terms and support guidance.

AstaBench paper:

- https://arxiv.org/pdf/2510.21652

## Operational behavior

- Keep searches targeted.
- Favor small, high-value retrieval steps over large bulk requests.
- If a question would require excessive calls, narrow the scope first.

## Reporting rule

Do not claim unrestricted commercial usability if the outputs were produced
using this access path.
