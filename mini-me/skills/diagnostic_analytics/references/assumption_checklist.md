# Diagnostic Assumption Checklist

Before reporting conclusions, check:

- Is the outcome variable valid and sufficiently observed?
- Are the main predictors reasonably measured?
- Are groups large enough for the chosen comparison?
- Are missing values concentrated in a way that can bias the result?
- Are outliers dominating the estimate?
- Are predictor variables highly collinear?
- Does the focal estimate change materially when controls are added?
- Is the time window appropriate for the claimed explanation?
- Are you using language that matches the design strength?
